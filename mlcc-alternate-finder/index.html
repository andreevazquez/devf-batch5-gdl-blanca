        How it decides. Capacitance and case size must match. Voltage rating can only go up. Dielectric can only go up (Y5V, X5R, X7R, C0G). Tolerance can only get tighter. The alternate must carry the grade the design requires, and grades aren’t a ladder: an AEC-Q200 automotive part can replace a commercial one, but neither qualifies for medical use without the manufacturer’s medical-grade series. A part that passes but changes something, like a higher voltage rating that can mean a taller part, is marked for review. A new manufacturer doesn’t change fit, but it does need an approved-vendor-list entry, so it’s noted. “Better than” shows only parts that improve at least one spec or the grade while giving nothing up.

        About the data. This demo catalog covers 0.1 µF, 1 µF, 10 µF and 100 pF ceramic capacitors from six manufacturers, in commercial and AEC-Q200 automotive grades; it has no medical-grade parts, so a medical search shows what’s required instead. Specs follow each manufacturer’s published part-numbering scheme; stock and lead times are simulated. Always confirm against the datasheet before approving a substitute.
